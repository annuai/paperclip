# Surface brief — "Flat" style (second skin)

Mode: Experience (the character is the product). Classic style stays untouched; Flat is an additional, switchable skin. Behaviour, animations, ids and pose system are shared.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A flat, poster-grade rendition of the same paperclip — personality carried by geometry and expression, not by gradients. Refuses the generic app-onboarding illustration look (pastel blobs, rounded-everything, faceless).

OWN-WORLD: Mid-century geometric flat illustration (Charley Harper-style minimal realism: solid shapes, one hard-edged shade per form, no outlines, no gradients) married to Swiss poster typography set in Oswald. Palette, all flat fills:
- Ink `#18202E` (brows, pupils, mouth, balloon/menu panel)
- Steel `#8394AB` wire (9.5 stroke), Steel-shade `#5F7088` (one hard shade run; also eye sockets), Glint `#E6ECF3` (one crisp highlight segment) — revised after finish review for legibility on light/dark wallpapers
- Marigold `#F2B33D` (mat top, accents), Ochre `#C98A1E` (mat front face), Marigold-light `#F7C964` (mat bands)
- Paper `#F4F1EA` (eye whites, balloon text), Coral `#E5705A` (tongue, tiny accents only)
- Ground shadow: flat ellipse, Ink at 14% opacity, no blur.

STORY: Same Paperclip, a sharper suit. The user flips "Style → Flat" and he re-appears instantly in the new skin mid-whatever-he-was-doing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Character standing on a marigold mat in flat 3/4 perspective (top face + ochre front face, two light bands), ink speech panel above-left with Oswald text and a flat triangular tail.

FORM: User-pinned direction ("elegant flat", Oswald). seed: none (pinned).

Signature: brows get bolder (ink, 7px, round caps) and drive emotion; the hard shade on the wire shifts with body tilt so the flat character still reads as an object.

Donor line (Harper): every form = one base colour + one hard-edged shade shape; no strokes as outlines.
Donor line (Swiss poster): typographic hierarchy by weight/case only; uppercase tracked Oswald for labels and attributions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
